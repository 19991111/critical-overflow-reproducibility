L1. Exact feedback, clocks and phase geometry
Let q=2m, X_q(t)=Delta_floor(q^2t)/q (linearly interpolated), h_n=n/(2q), mu0=h_n+X_q/2 and mu1=h_n-X_q/2. For PRODUCT the conditional cross feedback is exactly
 g_actual=P_n(L0<=R1)-P_n(L1<=R0).
Internal conditional drift cancels. Thus the conditional one-step Delta drift is epsilon g_actual. The limiting q^2-time drift coefficient is epsilon q, NOT epsilon a or an extra factor2. The martingale variance coefficient tends to1.
For the unique mean-locked family rho^u=(L_u,R_u), define
 H(u,v)=P(L_u<=R_v), independent draws,
 g(h,d)=H(h+d/2,h-d/2)-H(h-d/2,h+d/2),
 gbar(d)=int_0^1 g(h,d)dh.                                  (L1)
Integer translation gives period1 in h. The two community phases are h+d/2,h-d/2; they are not independent uniform phases. By the optional half-phase identity the period in h can also be taken1/2, but period1 is sufficient.
Since an integer comparison indicator is1-Lipschitz in either coordinate, independent optimal marginal couplings and d_1(rho^u,rho^v)=2|u-v| give
 |H(u,v)-H(u',v')|<=W1(L_u,L_u')+W1(R_v,R_v').
Adding both terms of G gives in particular
 |g(h,d)-g(h',d')|<=4|h-h'|+2|d-d'|.
For clarity the inequality used for G=H(u,v)-H(v,u) is
 |G(u,v)-G(u',v')|<=2|u-u'|+2|v-v'|.
It follows by adding the FOUR marginal W1 costs; no smooth density is assumed. Stochastic increase of both side laws makes g nonincreasing in d. It is odd in d and bounded by1. Therefore
 gbar is odd, nonincreasing, globally2-Lipschitz,
 gbar(0)=0, |gbar(d)|<=min(1,2|d|).                         (L2)
Full support of both orbit marginals makes g(h,d)<0 for every d>0: compare H(u,v) and H(v,u) through H(v,v), using strict stochastic increase and positive mass at all integer thresholds. Uniform exponential tails give g(h,d)->-sign(d) as |d|->infinity, uniformly in h. These are properties of the now-proved orbit, not an assumed sign model.

L2. Local linear restoring coefficient
Write a_k(u)=P(L_u=k), b_k(u)=P(R_u=k), and x,y for their tails. Time here is MEAN u, so the birth equations have factor2. Differentiating the bounded-rate laws and summing absolutely gives
 partial_1 H(u,u)=-2 sum_k a_k y_k b_k,
 partial_2 H(u,u)= 2 sum_k a_k x_k b_(k-1).
Hence
 gbar'(0)=-2 int_0^1 sum_k[a_k y_k b_k+a_k x_k b_(k-1)]du
          =-4 int_0^1 sum_k a_k y_k b_k du <0.               (L3)
The second equality integrates the derivative of the periodic H(u,u). All atoms are positive, so strict negativity is justified. Bounded rates give absolute convergence and continuity of these derivative series. Thus the finite-ratio critical drift is a continuous restoring function with a finite nonzero linear response near0, rather than a jump at0. No numerical value for L3 is claimed.

Proof of L4, including fast deterministic phase averaging
Let b_n=epsilon g_actual(n) and M_q=q^-1 sum(Delta increment-b_n). Its jumps are<=2/q and
 <M_q>(t)=q^-2 sum_(n<q^2t)(1-b_n^2)->t uniformly,
since |b_n|<=epsilon and epsilon=O(1/q). Each bracket jump is<=1/q^2 and the squared maximal martingale jump is<=4/q^2. Thus both expected-jump regularity conditions of Whitt's Theorem2.1(ii) hold, as does its predictable-variation hypothesis. The martingale functional CLT applies.
The exact product feedback differs from g(h_n,X_q(n/q^2)) by at most the sum of the four empirical-profile W1 errors. CRITICAL_TRACKING R2 makes its integrated expected error vanish. Multiplication by epsilon q remains bounded.
Now cut time into blocks of EXACTLY2q arrivals. The slow coordinate varies inside a block by
 E max |X_q(n)-X_q(block start)|<=2sqrt(2/q)+2epsilon,
by conditional-variance<=1 Doob and bounded drift. Freeze X at the block start. The phase h_n advances through the complete2q-point uniform grid modulo1; its average differs from the integral by at most2/q using the4-Lipschitz bound. The frozen/unfrozen errors for both g and gbar cost at most four times the displayed slow-coordinate variation. The last incomplete block costs O(1/q). Consequently
 E sup_(t<=T)|q^-2 sum_(n<tq^2)[g(h_n,X_q(n/q^2))-gbar(X_q(n/q^2))]|
 <=C(T+1)(q^-1/2+epsilon+q^-1)->0.                         (L5)
No ergodicity of an auxiliary phase process, nor independence of community phases, is invoked. The phase is a deterministic fast rotation.
Tightness follows from the bounded drift and martingale. Every subsequential limit solves L4; its globally Lipschitz drift gives a unique solution by the usual Brownian integral-equation/Picard argument. Thus convergence holds for the whole sequence. Doob also gives E sup|X_q|^2<=C(T,Lambda), so uniform integrability upgrades weak uniform-path convergence to W1 (for example via a Skorokhod coupling and truncation). Linear interpolation costs at most1/q. QED.

L4. The critical spill readout is not phase averaged
Define the unique deterministic function

 S_h(d)=sigma(h+d/2)+sigma(h-d/2).                          (L6)
The family is stochastically increasing, so sigma is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz. In mean clock,
 sigma'(u)=x_0(u)y_0(u),
because a positive-part increment occurs iff both candidates have load>=0. Thus sigma is convex, S_h is even and convex in d,1-Lipschitz in d and2-Lipschitz in h. Full support and mean0 imply sigma(0)>0. At zero imbalance the critical readout has a strictly positive internal-profile baseline2sigma(0); it is not |d|/2.

Scope and remaining non-effective content
The orbit, gbar and sigma are uniquely specified by the nonlinear fluid equations; no elementary closed form or certified numerical values are supplied. The deterministic attraction modulus has no explicit rate here, so this theorem supplies the asymptotic expected average error needed for the critical limit, not a practical finite-q precision certificate. The result requires uniform complete internal sampling and the global-left tie. Fixed cross couplings inherit it through the prior equivalence; load-aware adaptive cross responses are outside the product drift identification. No stationary interchange, arbitrary sparse-graph limit, or previously frozen empirical CDF accuracy is asserted.
