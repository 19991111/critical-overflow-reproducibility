A concrete conditional a-posteriori certificate and its still-missing numerical inputs

This lemma is independently derived here; it is not presented as an already evaluated orbit enclosure. Let v be a probability pair of EXACT combined mean0, pi the proved mean0 orbit point, and P the mean-time1 flow followed by integer shift-1. For an integerL>=1, suppose the following quantities have been VALIDATED:
 r_L>=d1(v,P^L v);
 tau_K>=sum_(states i outside C_K)|d_i|, d=tails(v)-tails(pi);
 the exact difference-chain transition U overL has U_i>=beta*nu for every i in the finite core C_K, where nu is one probability measure and0<beta<=1.
The common output shift is just a permutation of states and does not change l1. Then
 d1(v,pi)<=r_L/beta+2*tau_K.                              (N1)

Proof, including why the tail term is not divided bybeta.
The signed tail difference has totalmass0 by exact equality of combined means. Split it into d_C+d_O. Write the core transition as beta*nu plus a positive residual of columnmass1-beta. Because sum d_C=-sum d_O,
 ||Ud||1 <= beta*|sum d_C|+(1-beta)||d_C||1+||d_O||1
          <=(1-beta)||d||1+2beta||d_O||1.
Since P^L pi=pi, triangle inequality gives ||d||1<=r_L+||Ud||1, provingN1. This uses no unknown global spectral gap. If the candidate's mean is only known within delta of0, one must certify its matching-mean target or normalize exactly, then add2delta; otherwise N1's zero-totalmass step fails.

How beta could be validated without knowing pi numerically.
For the mean-clock difference generator, all off-diagonal rates are the arithmetic means of the two corresponding single-profile rates. Thus they are at least HALF the rates determined by the exact forward v(t) alone, while actual exit rates<=2. Define a known sub-Markov generator A(t) with these half-v off-diagonal rates and constant diagonal-2. Its positive evolution V is entrywise dominated by U, by Dyson expansion/positive comparison. A common finite terminal state j then gives beta=min_(i in C_K)V_(j,i)(L), if that minimum has a positive validated lower bound. Alternatively allocate finite ordered time intervals to explicit paths: e^(-2L) times products of the integrals of validated lower edge rates bounds each path probability. Merely evaluating floating rates is insufficient.
Both statements are rigorous conditional constructions. They do not claim that the crude path bound is numerically useful: a very small beta can amplify residual uncertainty severely. Choosing the core and a longer block must be justified together with a tail bound and may require sharper interval propagation.

How tau could be validated.
